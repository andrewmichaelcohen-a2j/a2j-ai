This file rebuilds it from what Andy has said and from what the repo already does. Every row marked "inferred" needs Andy's confirmation or correction. ## What exists today
- Committed docs (GLOSSARY, CJAC_ROADMAP, OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_LIMITATIONS, A2J_STACK_AND_CJAC_SCOPE) define THREE bands: B1 deterministic, B2 structured-subjective, B3 genuinely discretionary (a boundary marker, never a target).
- The playbook pilot uses THREE kinds of line (Rule, Guidance, Reference) and FOUR strength labels (Source-read, Lawyers differ, Not yet checked, Attested). These are labels on lines, not bands.

## What Andy said (from notes, Oct 29 and Oct 31)
- Oct 29: bands to sharpen were B1 deterministic obligations, B2 elements of a claim or defense, B3 particular-scenario judgment (UPL boundary), plus a self-help "common scenario" category.
- Oct 31: he approved a redefined four-band framework, assurance-based. Public output should stop at conditional guidance ("level 2"), except attested items and calculators that refuse unclear inputs.
- START-HERE: bands 1 and 2 are the statutory and rule text that can be looked up and quoted.

## Proposed four bands (inferred; confirm)
| Band | What it covers | Assurance it can earn | Public output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Deterministic rules and numbers: deadlines, limits, fees, forms | Source-read (quote verified in code); calculators tested | Stated plainly, with source and date read |
| 2 | Elements, burdens and procedure from statute and rule text | Source-read, or Lawyers differ where sources conflict | Stated with source; no outcome prediction |
| 3 | Common-scenario guidance ("talk to a lawyer before signing") | Attested by the named reviewer; no source can support it | Conditional guidance only |
| 4 | Particular-scenario judgment and UPL boundary (settle or fight, strategy, bankruptcy) | None; not answered | Clean hand-off to a lawyer or legal aid |

## Update (Nov 20, 12:05): related document found
Andy supplied `CJaC_Framework_Spec_v0.1_Nov_1_2026.pdf`. It is the framework spec (item schema, ladder, tests T1 to T9), not the band definitions. It uses the bands only as metadata: "Band (1 to 4), particularity (P0 to P3), release mode (A, A*, B, C, Refer)". It says answers are written at P0 to P2 for release and P3 is never released, which matches the Oct 31 "stop at conditional guidance (level 2)". So the missing definition pack should hold three things: the four bands, the particularity scale P0 to P3, and the release modes. None of the three is defined in the Framework Spec. The table above remains an inference until that pack is found.
