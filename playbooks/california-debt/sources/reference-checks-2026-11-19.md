## REF-SELFHELP
Name: California Courts Self-Help Center
Page: https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov
Finding: The site is part of the Judicial Branch of California ("California Courts | Self Help Guide"). It offers guidance on court papers by form number, steps for starting a case, general legal topics, fee waivers, forms, and help locating self-help centers that provide legal information and resources to people without a lawyer.
Does NOT establish: that every service is free. The page does not say so. Do not write "free" about this site unless a page says it.

## REF-LAWHELPCA
Name: LawHelpCA
Page: https://www.lawhelpca.org/about-us
Finding: LawHelpCA is California's legal resource and referral website. It has self-help information on common legal issues and a directory of pre-screened organizations that provide free or low-cost legal advice and representation. The Legal Aid Association of California (LAAC) maintains it. It describes itself as a statewide resource, searchable by location or service area.
Does NOT establish: which counties or regions each listed organization covers.
Note: the home page www.lawhelpca.org could not be fetched by the tool; the About page was.
