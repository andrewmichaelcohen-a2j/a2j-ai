Unless another statute says otherwise, a summons must tell the defendant to file a written response with the court within 30 days after the summons is served on the defendant.
To count the days, leave out the first day and include the last day. If the last day is a holiday, leave it out too: the deadline then moves to the next day that is not a holiday. For this purpose, all day on Saturdays counts as a holiday.
Form SUM-100 says: "You have 30 CALENDAR DAYS after this summons and legal papers are served on you to file a written response at this court and have a copy served on the plaintiff."
