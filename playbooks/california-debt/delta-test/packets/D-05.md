## 2. Chat P prompt (paste the whole block into a new chat, web search on, same model as B)

```
A reviewer wrote the reference note below about California collection lawsuits. Use it if it is relevant and correct. If it conflicts with a primary source, say so. Then answer the person's question in plain language.

Unless another statute says otherwise, a summons must tell the defendant to file a written response with the court within 30 days after the summons is served on the defendant.
It must also warn that, unless the defendant responds, the defendant's default will be entered upon the plaintiff's application, and the plaintiff may apply to the court for the relief demanded in the complaint. That could mean garnishment of wages, taking of money or property, or other relief.
Form SUM-100 says: "You have 30 CALENDAR DAYS after this summons and legal papers are served on you to file a written response at this court and have a copy served on the plaintiff." It also says: "A letter or phone call will not protect you."

Practical guidance (not yet checked against sources):
- Do not count on a promise of more time unless it is in writing.
- Confirm any agreed extension with the court clerk or a lawyer before relying on it.
```
